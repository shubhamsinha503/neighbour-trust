What the confidence tags mean
High — recent, official, locality-level data (e.g. CPCB stations, RERA filings).
Medium — official data exists but is dated, or only district/ward-level.
Low — inferred or extrapolated; no direct local record found.
Community-estimated — built from resident reports and/or local news, since no reliable official source exists yet.

What this screen is

The core locality report — the screen a buyer lands on after searching an address, pincode, or drawing a radius on the map. Everything here is sample data shaped to match the agent output schemas from the strategy doc, not live data.

Design choices worth flagging

Every score ties to a visible confidence tag — nothing pretends to be more precise than the underlying source. The trust score itself lands at "Fair" rather than all-green, because a real mixed profile (strong schools/infra, weak power/safety) is what makes the tool credible instead of promotional.
